How to Play

Touch controls

Tap a unit, then tap an adjacent hex to move or attack. Drag the map to pan. Pinch or use +/− to zoom. Tap a city to choose its production.

Grow your empire

Cities collect food, production, gold, science, and culture from nearby terrain. Settlers found new cities at least three hexes apart. Research unlocks stronger projects.

Win the campaign

Capture every rival city for a Domination Victory, complete Future Flight for a Science Victory, or hold the highest score after turn 80.

Command Console

The console is deliberately available in offline single-player. Its commands are stored with the save so you always know whether a toggle is active.
